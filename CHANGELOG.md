# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- **Bone meal spreads spore blossoms** along the ceiling, the same way it spreads dead bushes, also from dispensers.
- **Uneven ground and ceilings**: dead bushes, spore blossoms and firefly bushes also spread to a spot one block higher
  or lower beside them.
- **Guide book**: new Spore Blossom chapter, a line about firefly bushes in Getting Started.

## 0.1.0
- **Bone meal spreads dead bushes** the way it spreads firefly bushes, also from the offhand and from dispensers.
- **Dead bushes drop themselves** with any tool or by hand; shears are no longer needed.
- **Guide book** with the dead bush's chapter, in English and German.
